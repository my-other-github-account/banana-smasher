# Canonical Backpack six-phase fan-in

Status: **accepted and merged through phase 6**. The semantic fan-in tip is
`5dacce03b370b40245f4ab22ce737f0d3156eb50`, the exact `origin/main` observed
after PR #23 merged on 2026-08-06.

## Final accepted surface

- The standardized same-instrument DwarfStar/IQ3 HumanEval comparison is
  retained with exact artifact and target-only serving-stack scope. It does not
  establish quantization causality.
- Backpack has one composable public construction surface: provider generation,
  exact receipt pricing, prediction, materialization, verification, and the
  `build_backpack(...)` / `smash backpack build` orchestration path.
- The built-in physical menu retains native MXFP4, QTIP2/QTIP2.5/QTIP3, and
  fixed D4 K2048/K4096. Shared activation artifacts are charged once by the
  exact solver.
- The standalone `physical-repair` wheel entry point retains canonical QTIP2
  decode/geometry validation, immutable decoded state, TLUT-only training,
  physical-cycle sentinel reset, and explicit CPU-reference accounting.
- The stock-vLLM pip-plugin closure retains native planes outside CUDA graph
  capture, materialized D4, fixed and mixed QTIP wires, FP8 repair-scale
  folding, the accepted QTIP2.5 specialization matrix, FlashInfer registration,
  signed K3 containers, public pack layouts, and route-padding normalization.
- Contextual Backpack retains BQ23, virtual and exact64 bindings,
  measured-monotonic selection, contextual prepare/materialize/record/value/
  solve stages, local compute inputs, and explicit collision-free fan-in
  staging outside pricing and solve APIs.
- Authentic QTIP1 remains canonical `L16/K1/V1`. QTIP1.5 remains an exact,
  deterministic 50/50 composition of QTIP1 `K1/V1` and QTIP2 `K2/V2`, distinct
  from QTIP2.5. Generic quality-fitted pre-encoded wire materialization,
  transactional verification, and `QtipWireConsumer` runtime decode are
  accepted at exact `1.5` code bit/weight.

The QTIP1.5 acceptance is a generic wire/runtime-consumer result. Direct
stock-vLLM K1/V1 kernel dispatch remains absent and is not a serving or
full-model quality claim.

## Explicit exclusions

The fan-in keeps reviewed semantics, not every branch or campaign artifact that
preceded them. The canonical result excludes:

- conflicting/superseded PR #7 and PR #16 trees as merge authorities; PR #19
  selectively retained their accepted accelerated-QTIP and provider semantics;
- failed, partial, cancelled, or nonterminal evaluation variants, including the
  QTIP1.5 paired exact64 evaluation cancelled as superseded and stale PARTIAL
  report commit `483b908`;
- handoff-only artifacts, campaign-only allocation gates, and draft/rollback
  variants excluded by the reviewed integrations;
- duplicated backend-base or unrelated source-worktree ancestry;
- the retired fixed 50/50 QTIP2/QTIP3 "QTIP2.5 average" as a per-cell codec
  tier; it is not the accepted modern QTIP2.5 specialization;
- any runtime, build, install, test, development, import, subprocess, path,
  wheel, or generated-code dependency on historical/private trees; and
- any direct stock-vLLM K1/V1 serving, hardware-performance, or full-model
  quality claim for QTIP1/QTIP1.5.

All source branches and worktrees remain as evidence; exclusion from the
canonical semantic fan-in is not branch deletion or archival.
