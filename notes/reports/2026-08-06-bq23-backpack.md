## Name and lifecycle

**BQ23** is the short name for the dynamic Backpack whose physical option menu contains native MXFP4, QTIP2, QTIP3, D4-K2048, and D4-K4096 rather than treating the retired fixed-average QTIP2.5 portfolio as a fictitious per-cell codec tier. “23” names the dynamic QTIP2/QTIP3 backbone; it does not mean D4 is absent.

Use these names consistently:

| Stage | Short name | Deployable family name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-repair | **BQ23-PRE** | `BANANA-SMASHER-BQ23-PRE` | Sealed physical assignment before repair |
| Update12 repair | **BQ23-U12** | `BANANA-SMASHER-BQ23-U12` | The BQ23 assignment after Update12 repair and exact64 rescoring |

`BQ23-U12` must not be presented as measured until its repair artifact has a terminal physical exact64 receipt. “BQ23” names the assignment family; `PRE` and `U12` name the measured lifecycle stage.

## Relation to the historical F521/U012 DQ-BQ Backpack

BQ23 is **not** the same assignment lineage as the historical F521/U012 DQ-BQ Backpack. The authenticated historical assignment (`f521cf07...`, canonical map `786b01a3...`) contained:

| Historical tier | Selected cells | Share of 22,016 |
|---|---:|---:|
| QTIP2 | 2,266 | 10.29% |
| QTIP3 | 14,979 | 68.04% |
| D4-K1024 | 197 | 0.89% |
| D4-K2048 | 2,610 | 11.86% |
| D4-K4096 | 1,910 | 8.68% |
| Native MXFP4 | 54 | 0.25% |
| **All D4** | **4,717** | **21.43%** |

That historical map really was D4-rich. Its original solve authority also explicitly labels it `OPERATOR_AUTHORIZED_BASELINE_PREVIEW`, records a failed retrodiction gate, and requires a definitive rerun after the full seals. It remains valid identity evidence for the accepted historical post-repair artifact, but it is not proof that a refreshed full-wire solve must reproduce the same tier counts.

The modern BQ23 menu differs materially:

- D4-K1024 was not present because no fresh modern K1024 option row was admitted.
- A subsequent wire-accounting correction added the required D4 scales/shared VQ payload, fixing approximately 0.25 bpw of prior D4 underpricing.
- Under the corrected per-cell ledger, D4-K4096 was strictly dominated by QTIP3 for all 22,016 cells: higher predicted damage in every class and 376,830–380,926 more per-cell payload bytes.
- D4-K2048 had higher equal-class predicted damage than QTIP3 for all 22,016 cells. It was smaller than QTIP3 for 11,008 cells, but the QTIP2/QTIP3 package tradeoff won the capped solve; 11,027 D4-K2048 rows were individually Pareto dominated.

Therefore zero D4 is explainable from the corrected ledger and is not, by itself, evidence of a solver filter bug. It is nevertheless a **real product-lineage change**, not a cosmetic count difference. BQ23-PRE must not silently replace or be described as the same product as the historical D4-rich F521/U012 DQ-BQ artifact. Promotion should be based on its own measured post-repair result. D4-K1024 should return only after a fresh modern anchor and full-wire byte row are available.

## What the retired “QTIP2.5 average” was

The retired average was **not a native 2.5-bit representation**. It was a static whole-model portfolio whose members were physical QTIP2 or physical QTIP3 artifacts:

- QTIP2: `11,008`
- QTIP3: `11,008`
- total: `22,016`

The sealed assignment map is authoritative. “Alternating” must not be interpreted as a universal periodic codec rule; it was a fixed per-layer/per-expert/per-tensor map. Its whole-model average rate did not make it a new per-cell tier.

A dynamic Backpack can place QTIP3 where its measured marginal benefit is highest and QTIP2 where its damage is lowest. A fixed portfolio is only one possible assignment. Under matching constraints and a correct physical objective, making both physical options independently available cannot make the optimum worse. Proxy valuation can still select a worse candidate, which is why physical exact64 selection remains mandatory.

## Decision

1. Retire the static 50/50 QTIP2/QTIP3 “QTIP2.5 average” effort.
2. Keep **BQ23** as the dynamic native-MXFP4/QTIP2/QTIP3/D4 Backpack family.
3. Call the sealed pre-repair result **BQ23-PRE**.
4. Apply Update12 repair only to the selected BQ23 assignment, then exact64-score it as **BQ23-U12**.
5. Deploy the repaired artifact as **BANANA-SMASHER-BQ23-U12** only after its physical terminal receipt passes.
6. Reserve future “QTIP2.5” naming for genuinely modern QTIP2.5 mechanisms, not the retired fixed average.
7. Retain D4-K2048 and D4-K4096 as first-class options in future BQ23 solves even though BQ23-PRE selected zero D4 cells.
